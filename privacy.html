Privacy

The short version: our apps collect nothing at all.

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Our apps have no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers and no accounts, and none of them opens a network connection.

What stays on your device

Some of our apps remember a few things on your device, so they can pick up where you left off. This is all of it, app by app:

Circell iPhone
Remembers which level you reached, your sound and music switches, and whether you pinned day or night.
Confitura iPhone
Remembers your best ever, the best of the week, and day or night.
Brillantor Mac
Remembers nothing between launches.
Segell Mac
Remembers which certificate you signed with last and whether you wanted the signature to show on the page. Your documents, certificates and keys never leave your Mac: the private key stays in the keychain, and a .p12 file is read in memory and never stored. Segell does not even have permission to use the network.

None of it ever leaves your device. On iPhone it goes when you delete the app. On a Mac, macOS may keep it in your Library folder after you delete the app; it is still only on your Mac.

Third parties

There are none. We use no SDKs from anyone else. If you buy an app, Apple handles the payment and tells us nothing about you beyond anonymous sales totals.

This website

This page is static, hosted on GitHub Pages. There are no cookies and no analytics. GitHub keeps standard server logs of requests, which we do not read.

Children

Because we collect nothing, our apps are safe for any age.
