Privacy

The short version: our apps collect nothing at all.

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Circell and Brillantor have no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers and no accounts. Neither app opens a network connection.

What stays on your device

Circell remembers which level you reached, and your sound and music switches. Brillantor remembers nothing between launches. This data never leaves your device and is deleted with the app.

Third parties

There are none. We use no SDKs from anyone else. If you buy an app, Apple handles the payment and tells us nothing about you beyond anonymous sales totals.

This website

This page is static, hosted on GitHub Pages. There are no cookies and no analytics. GitHub keeps standard server logs of requests, which we do not read.

Children

Because we collect nothing, our apps are safe for any age.
